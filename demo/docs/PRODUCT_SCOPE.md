# Product A — Demo Scope & Page Map

## Positioning

A premium, editorial cultural technology website for Western users interested in Chinese metaphysical traditions. The experience is a transparent personal chart and annual cultural analysis service, not an “AI fortune-telling” shortcut.

## User journey

1. Landing page: value proposition, traditions, methodology and annual report preview.
2. Birth details: Gregorian date, local birth time, birthplace, birth time zone, sex used for traditional rules and time accuracy.
3. Free chart workspace: BaZi, Zi Wei and Heluo. No account or email required to calculate a chart in the intended production product.
4. Annual report preview: user selects a year and sees the actual scope, limits, report language and price before ordering.
5. Email and order details: email confirmation and required legal consents.
6. Order review / payment integration: production must verify payment server-side before report generation.
7. Report generation and QA: only production-admitted capabilities and evidence-backed statements may enter the report.
8. Email delivery: transaction email with secure, expiring report access link; report contents and birth details are not exposed in the subject line.

## Free chart modules

- BaZi: Four Pillars and supported structural details.
- Zi Wei Dou Shu: Twelve-palace chart and supported star / transformation relationships.
- Heluo: Pre-Heaven, Later-Heaven and annual hexagrams with line-level exploration.
- Basic major-cycle context may be shown only where the calculation capability is admitted.

## Paid annual report

- One birth profile × one selected year × one report version.
- Stable dimensions: work and direction; finance and resources; relationships and family; wellbeing and balance; people and environment.
- Systems: BaZi, Blind School, Lu Ming, Zi Wei and Heluo. Blind School is a supporting cross-reference lens, not a separate free dashboard.
- Cross-reference must preserve consensus, differences and unresolved conflicts.
- Original chart, major cycles, Zi Wei limits and minor cycles are concise background, not the report's bulk.
- Content depth is conditional on active, admitted rules. No special timing signal means no invented turning point.

## Excluded from Product A

NFC, bracelets, touch activation, daily lifestyle prompts, daily subscription usage and the Product B “visitor / logged-in / subscriber” model.
